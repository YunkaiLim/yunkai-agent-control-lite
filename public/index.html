THE PRODUCT THESIS

One calm surface for a messy local AI stack.

01

Agent presence

Know which developer CLIs are installed and which uniquely identifiable local processes are running.

02

Local runtimes

Check local model services without opening three terminals or exposing model inventory to a cloud dashboard.

03

Trust, not vibes

Separate “process exists” from “authenticated”, “authorized”, and “task accepted”. No fake green READY light.

04

Grow only when earned

MCP inventory, provider management, and gated lifecycle actions stay out until users prove they want them.
